Let:

  • \(S\) be the number of points the proposer sends,
  • \(M\) be the realized multiplier, and
  • \(R\) be the number of points the responder returns.

The proposer's round payoff is:

\[ \mathit{payoff}_P = 20 - S + R \]

The responder's round payoff is:

\[ \mathit{payoff}_R = S M - R \]

After the experiment, one period is selected at random for payment.

A period consists of two rounds.

Both rounds from the selected period determine trust-game earnings.

Each point earned in those rounds is worth ${{ trust_point_value }}.

For example, 22 points in one round of the selected period would be worth ${{ example_22_point_payment }}.

Practice rounds and unselected periods do not affect payment.
