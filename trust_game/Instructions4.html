The proposer's round payoff is:

\[ \text{proposer round points} = 20 - \text{points sent} + \text{points returned} \]

The responder's round payoff is:

\[ \text{responder round points} = \text{points sent} \times \text{realized multiplier} - \text{points returned} \]

After the experiment, one period is selected at random for payment. A period consists of two rounds, and both rounds from the selected period determine trust-game earnings.

Each point earned in those rounds is worth ${{ trust_point_value }}. For example, 22 points in one round of the selected period would be worth ${{ example_22_point_payment }}. Practice rounds and unselected periods do not affect payment.
