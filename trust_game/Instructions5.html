In every round, after seeing how many points the responder returned, the proposer reports \(X\), the probability (from 0% to 100%) that the low multiplier was used.

For example, \(X = 70\%\) means you believe there is a 70% chance that the low multiplier was used.

The same period selected for trust-game payment is also used for belief payment.

A period consists of two rounds.

The proposer's report from each round in the selected period is evaluated for a ${{ belief_prize }} belief prize.

For each report in the selected period, your report and the actual multiplier determine your chance \(W\) of winning that round's belief prize.

If the multiplier was low:

\[ W = 100 - \frac{(100-X)^2}{100}. \]

If the multiplier was high:

\[ W = 100 - \frac{X^2}{100}. \]

The computer then draws \(Y\).

The number \(Y\) is equally likely to be any integer from 0 through 100.

You receive the belief prize when:

\[ Y \leq W. \]

Higher values of \(W\) give you a better chance of receiving the prize.

Reporting your true belief about the low multiplier gives you the best chance of receiving it.

At the end, everyone answers demographic questions about themselves.
