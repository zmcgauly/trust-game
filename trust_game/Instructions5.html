In every round, after seeing how many points the responder returned, the proposer reports the probability (from 0% to 100%) that the low multiplier was used. For example, reporting 70% means you believe there is a 70% chance that the low multiplier was used.

The same period selected for trust-game payment is also used for belief payment. A period consists of two rounds, and the proposer's report from each round in the selected period is evaluated for a ${{ belief_prize }} belief prize. For each report in the selected period, your report and the actual multiplier determine your chance of winning that round's belief prize.

If the multiplier was low:

\[ \text{winning chance} = 100 - \frac{(100 - \text{reported probability})^2}{100}. \]

If the multiplier was high:

\[ \text{winning chance} = 100 - \frac{(\text{reported probability})^2}{100}. \]

The computer then draws a number that is equally likely to be any integer from 0 through 100. You receive the belief prize when:

\[ \text{random draw} \leq \text{winning chance}. \]

Higher values of the winning chance give you a better chance of receiving the prize. Reporting your true belief about the low multiplier gives you the best chance of receiving it.

These instructions are for Part 1. Once Part 1 is completed, you will receive the instructions for Part 2.
