  • In every round, after seeing how many points the responder returned, the proposer reports the probability that the low multiplier was used.
  • The report can be any whole number from 0% to 100%.
  • For example, reporting 70% means you believe there is a 70% chance that the low multiplier was used.
  • The same period selected for trust-game payment is also used for belief payment.
  • A period consists of {{ rounds_per_period }} rounds.
  • The proposer's report from each round in the selected period is evaluated for a ${{ belief_prize }} belief prize.
  • For each report in the selected period, your report and the actual multiplier determine your chance of winning that round's belief prize.
  • If the multiplier was low:

\[ \text{winning chance} = 100 - \frac{(100 - \text{reported probability})^2}{100}. \]

  • If the multiplier was high:

\[ \text{winning chance} = 100 - \frac{(\text{reported probability})^2}{100}. \]

  • Example using different numbers: suppose the multiplier was low and you reported 80%.
  • First calculate 100 - 80 = 20.
  • Then square 20 to get 400.
  • Then divide 400 by 100 to get 4.
  • Then calculate 100 - 4 = 96, so your chance of winning the belief prize would be 96%.
  • The computer then draws a random number from 0 through 100.
  • You receive the belief prize when:

\[ \text{random draw} \leq \text{winning chance}. \]

  • Higher values of the winning chance give you a better chance of receiving the prize.
  • Reporting your true belief about the low multiplier gives you the best chance of receiving it.
