In every round, after seeing how many points the responder returned, the proposer reports the probability, from 0% to 100%, that the multiplier was {{ low_multiplier }}. For example, reporting 70% means you believe there is a 70% chance that the multiplier was {{ low_multiplier }}.

Each real-round belief report is evaluated for a ${{ belief_prize }} belief prize. Your reported probability and the actual multiplier determine your chance of winning that round's belief prize.

If the multiplier was {{ low_multiplier }}:

\[ \text{winning chance} = 100 - \frac{(100 - \text{reported probability})^2}{100}. \]

If the multiplier was {{ large_multiplier }}:

\[ \text{winning chance} = 100 - \frac{(\text{reported probability})^2}{100}. \]

The computer then draws a number that is equally likely to be any integer from 0 through 100. You receive the belief prize when:

\[ \text{random draw} \leq \text{winning chance}. \]

Higher values of the winning chance give you a better chance of receiving the prize. Reporting your true belief about the {{ low_multiplier }} multiplier gives you the best chance of receiving it.
