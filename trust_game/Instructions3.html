  • In every round, the proposer receives 20 points.
  • The proposer chooses a whole number from 0 to 20 to send to the responder.
  • The amount sent in your group is multiplied by either {{ low_multiplier }} or {{ high_multiplier }}.
    • {{ if picture_condition }}
  • When making decisions about how many points to send to one another, both players will be able to see their own picture and the picture of their partner.
    • {{ endif }}
  • The computer selects a number between 0 and 100.
  • If the number is less than {{ low_multiplier_probability_percent }}, the computer selects the low multiplier of {{ low_multiplier }}.
  • Otherwise, it selects the high multiplier of {{ high_multiplier }}.
  • In this way, it is as if the high multiplier is selected {{ high_multiplier_probability_percent }}% of the time and the low multiplier is selected {{ low_multiplier_probability_percent }}% of the time.
  • The proposer does not see the realized multiplier.
  • The responder receives the amount sent by the proposer, the multiplier, and the resulting number of points from that multiplication.
  • The responder then chooses a whole number of points to return, from 0 up to the amount available.
