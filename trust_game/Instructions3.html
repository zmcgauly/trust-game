In every round, the proposer receives 20 points and chooses a whole number from 0 to 20 to send to the responder. The amount sent in your group is multiplied by either {{ low_multiplier }} or {{ high_multiplier }}.

The high multiplier is selected with probability {{ high_multiplier_probability_percent }}%, and the low multiplier is selected with probability {{ low_multiplier_probability_percent }}%. The proposer does not see the realized multiplier.

The responder sees the sent amount, the realized multiplier, and the resulting number of available points. The responder then chooses a whole number of points to return, from 0 up to the amount available.
