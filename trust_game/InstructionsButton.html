Instructions Summary

  • Your proposer or responder role remains fixed.
  • There are two unpaid practice rounds, followed by {{ total_periods }} periods with rotating partners.
  • Each period consists of {{ rounds_per_period }} rounds.
  • The proposer sends 0 to 20 points.
  • The amount is multiplied by {{ low_multiplier }} or {{ high_multiplier }}, and the high multiplier occurs with probability {{ high_multiplier_probability_percent }}% in each round.
  • The proposer earns \(\text{proposer round points} = 20 - \text{points sent} + \text{points returned}\).
  • The responder earns \(\text{responder round points} = \text{points sent} \times \text{realized multiplier} - \text{points returned}\).
  • One period is selected at random for payment.
  • Trust-game points from all rounds in that period are paid at ${{ trust_point_value }} per point, and the ${{ participation_fee }} show-up payment is added to these earnings.
  • After seeing each return, proposers report the probability that the low multiplier was used.
  • All reports from the selected period are evaluated.
  • For each report, the reported probability and the actual multiplier determine a winning chance.
  • The computer draws a random number from 0 through 100.
  • That round's ${{ belief_prize }} belief prize is paid when \(\text{random draw} \leq \text{winning chance}\).
  • There is a calculator for you to use should you need it. Simply press the calculator button in the upper right hand of your screen to access it.
