Instructions Summary

Your proposer or responder role remains fixed.

There are two unpaid practice rounds.

The practice rounds are followed by {{ total_periods }} periods with rotating partners.

Each period consists of two rounds.

The proposer sends 0–20 points.

The amount is multiplied by {{ low_multiplier }} or {{ high_multiplier }}.

The high multiplier occurs with probability {{ high_multiplier_probability_percent }}% in each round.

Let \(S\) be the amount sent, \(M\) the realized multiplier, and \(R\) the amount returned.

The proposer earns \(\mathit{payoff}_P = 20 - S + R\) round points.

The responder earns \(\mathit{payoff}_R = S M - R\) round points.

One period is selected at random for payment.

Trust-game points from both rounds in that period are paid at ${{ trust_point_value }} per point.

The ${{ participation_fee }} show-up payment is added to these earnings.

After seeing each return, proposers report \(X\), the probability that the low multiplier was used.

Both reports from the selected period are evaluated.

For each report, \(X\) and the actual multiplier determine a winning chance \(W\).

The computer draws \(Y\), which is equally likely to be any integer from 0 through 100.

That round's ${{ belief_prize }} belief prize is paid when \(Y \leq W\).
